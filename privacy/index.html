SkipLine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What we collect

SkipLine has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name or email address.

  • Location (optional): if you allow it, your approximate location is used on your device to show nearby places. It is not sent to us.
  • Saved places and trip plans: stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. We cannot read them.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: basic events such as which screens or partner links are opened (for example, a partner name and a city). They contain no name, email, or device identifier that identifies you.

AI concierge

The concierge runs on your device with Apple Intelligence. Your questions are not sent to our servers.

Partner links

SkipLine earns a commission when you book through links to partners such as GetYourGuide, Agoda, Aviasales, Airalo, Radical Storage, AirHelp, EconomyBookings, QEEQ and Welcome Pickups. This costs you nothing extra. When you open a link, the partner's own website and privacy policy apply.

Data sources

Places and photos come from open data (Wikidata, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, Wikimedia Commons, Pexels).

Children

SkipLine is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can turn off location in iOS Settings, turn off iCloud sync in the app, and delete the app to remove local data.
