Common questions

How do I turn off location?

iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → SkipLine.

How do I sync my saved places?

Turn on iCloud sync in the app. Your data stays in your private iCloud account.

A partner link does not work.

Send us the place name and the partner shown and we will check it.
